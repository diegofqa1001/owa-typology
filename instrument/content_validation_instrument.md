# Content-validation instrument — confirmatory expert phase (English summary of v3.1)

The authoritative version of the instrument is the Spanish file
[`instrumento_validacion_v3_1.md`](instrumento_validacion_v3_1.md), which matches
Appendix A of the doctoral thesis. This file is an English summary with the same
numbering of dimensions (Table 3.1) and profiles (Table 3.3).

Status: the instrument was pre-tested with a synthetic panel of twelve agents
(a control simulation of the protocol). The human expert round awaits approval by
the institutional ethics committee; its results are not part of the thesis.

## Response format
Five-point Likert scale: 1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree.

## Decision rules (fixed in advance)
- Median ≥ 4 and CVR ≥ 0.78 → validated with minor adjustments
- Median ≥ 4 and CVR < 0.78 → second round with targeted feedback
- Median < 4 → major revision of the item

CVR = (n_e − N/2)/(N/2), with n_e the number of panelists rating the item 4 or 5
(Lawshe, 1975); threshold 0.78 (Zamanzadeh et al., 2015). Concordance: Kendall's W
corrected for ties (Schmidt, 1997) and W_obs/W_max (Meijering et al., 2013).

## Section A — Behavioral dimensions (7 dimensions × 4 criteria = 28 items)
D1 Risk tolerance; D10 Ambiguity tolerance; D5 Loss aversion (inverse);
D7 Emotional regulation; D4 Financial self-efficacy; D8 Investment horizon;
D12 Perceived social influence (inverse).
Criteria: (i) clarity; (ii) relevance; (iii) differentiation (conceptual
independence); (iv) operationalizability.

## Section B — Prototypical profiles (8 profiles × 4 criteria = 32 items)
P1 Guardian, P2 Sentinel, P3 Pragmatist, P6 Analyst, P4 Strategist,
P5 Adventurer, P7 Innovator, P8 Visionary (ordered by orness; octile anchors
0.0625 … 0.9375).
Criteria: (i) internal coherence of the fuzzy linguistic vector;
(ii) differentiation from neighboring profiles; (iii) representativeness of a real
investor archetype; (iv) usefulness for adaptive recommendation.

Priority items (fewer than half of the synthetic-panel replicates reached
consensus): differentiation of D10 and D12, relevance of D12, differentiation of
the Pragmatist and the Adventurer, and usefulness of the Adventurer.

## Section C — Global system (5 items)
C1 coverage of the seven dimensions; C2 coverage of the eight profiles;
C3 superiority over the conservative/moderate/aggressive triad; C4 adequacy of the
fuzzy linguistic formalization; C5 coherence of the RIM calibration (profile → orness).

## Panel composition
Ten to twelve experts. Subpanel A (content, n ≈ 6): behavioral finance, financial
psychology, investor profiling. Subpanel B (method, n ≈ 6): OWA operators, fuzzy
logic, recommender systems.

## Ethics and data protection
Administration requires approval by the institutional ethics committee and each
expert's informed consent under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 on personal data
protection. Consent covers purpose, voluntary participation, confidentiality of
individual responses and aggregate-only reporting. The list of experts and the
consent records are kept under the approved protocol and are not published here.
